          In the App

          Reminders

          A banner in the app before your leave time and again when it's time to leave, and a notification when the app isn't on screen. Only while the app is open: a locked phone, or the browser in the background, won't get them.

          Early Reminder

          Reminder Chime

          A short chime when a reminder appears; phones that allow it also buzz.

          Push Notifications

          Push Notifications

          Reminders before each class starts, even when the app is closed or the screen is locked, sent by Chambana Nav's server. They know your classes, but not your live leave time. Needs you signed in; class changes reach them within about 15 minutes.

          Places

          Saved places come first in the From list on the Today page. Save one by picking a spot on the map, or with Save on a search result; Home is set the first time you tap Home.
